# About

## Purpose

Introduces the professional background, development focus, and problem-solving approach behind the portfolio.

## Architecture

- `About.jsx` controls the section structure.
- `AboutHighlights.jsx` renders highlight cards.
- `portfolio.js` stores the About content.
- `about.css` controls presentation and responsive behavior.

## Responsive Behavior

- Two-column story layout on larger screens.
- Single-column layout below 820px.
- Four highlight cards on desktop.
- Two highlight cards on tablets.
- One highlight card on mobile.

## Future Enhancements

- Scroll-triggered animation
- Technology icons
- Downloadable résumé link
- Professional timeline
